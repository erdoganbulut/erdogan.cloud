## Summary

I started software development with C++ at the age of 11 or 12 out of personal
interest, then continued with Visual Basic and C#. When I was 13 or 14, I
started learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP to build my own website. Since
then, I have focused on frontend development.

When I started high school, I also began working as a freelance developer,
mainly on frontend and sometimes backend projects. I studied Web Programming
at an Anatolian vocational high school. During my final year, I worked as a
frontend developer intern.

After high school, I started working full-time. I then began university and
chose the evening programme so I could continue working full-time while
studying.

I started my career in digital and advertising agencies. Working on many
projects in a short period helped me develop my technical skills. I later
moved to product companies and worked on software products.

I am currently a co-founder of Quapra, where I work as an AI & Frontend
Architect.
